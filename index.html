Welcome to Clay

Onboarding at Clay is a cohort experience — and one of the most Clay things we do. Every new Claymate travels to Clay HQ in New York City, meets their new hire class on day one, bonds with their team, learns how we work, and builds — with Clay the product, and actual clay itself 🏺. We're excited to go on this journey with you!
✈️
GETTING READY

Two weeks before

  • Book onboarding travel (if outside NYC)
    • We invite every Claymate to onboard at Clay HQ for their first two weeks.
    • Week two is optional, but highly encouraged so that you can soak up as much culture as you can.
    • You will receive a Navan invite two weeks before your start date so you can book your flight and hotel.
    • If you have not received an invite two weeks before you start, please contact onboarding@clay.com
    • When booking, please refer to our travel policy
  • Start exploring Clay (encouraged)
    • You'll get access to Clay 101, our Uplimit learning platform, two weeks before your start date — self-paced, about 10 hours. This invite will come to your personal email.
      Join the Clay Community Slack to get inspired.
    • Use your personal email for now.
💻
Week before

The week before

Thursday
  • Set up your Clay email — you'll get access to your Clay email on the Thursday before you start. Instructions will be sent to your personal email.
    • If you don't have access by Friday, please let us know at onboarding@clay.com.
  • Accept Slack & Notion invites after logging into your Clay email.
  • Submit the onboarding survey — this goes into our Claymate Notion database so your fellow Claymates can get to know you.
Friday afternoon
  • Download Parkday to order lunch. Menu drops Sunday night — order by 9am Monday.
Sunday
  • Order lunch on Parkday before 9am. We recommend ordering for the whole week.
  • For US-based Claymates, gather I-9 documents to bring Monday. Acceptable documents →
  • Plan to arrive by 9:30am. Dress code: wear what makes you feel like you.
